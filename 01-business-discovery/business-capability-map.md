# Business Capability Map

## Purpose

This document identifies the core business capabilities required for ASHREI Facility Services to operate efficiently, scale sustainably and support future digital transformation initiatives.

Business capabilities describe **what the organization must be able to do**, independent of people, departments or technology.

---

# Strategic Capabilities

- Business Strategy
- Financial Management
- Risk Management
- Compliance Management
- Digital Transformation

---

# Business Analysis

The current assessment indicates that ASHREI already performs most of these capabilities manually.

As the organization grows, these capabilities should become standardized, documented and supported by appropriate technology.
