# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Gap Analysis is to compare ASHREI's current operating environment with its desired future state.

The analysis identifies the differences (gaps) between existing business capabilities and the organization's future objectives. These findings will guide future phases of the Digital Transformation & AI Governance Program.

## Summary

The analysis indicates that ASHREI's greatest gaps are not technological but operational.

Most recommended improvements involve establishing standardized business processes before introducing additional technology.

This approach reduces operational risk and supports sustainable growth.
