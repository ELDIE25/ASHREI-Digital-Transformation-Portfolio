# Current State Assessment

## Purpose

This assessment documents how ASHREI currently operates before implementing new technology or governance processes.

---

## Operations

Cleaning schedules are coordinated manually using Microsoft Teams, phone calls and spreadsheets.

There is no standardized workflow for assigning work or tracking completion.

---

## IT Support

Employees report technical issues directly to supervisors using calls, text messages or Microsoft Teams.

No ticketing platform exists.

---

## Knowledge Management

Business knowledge is largely informal.

Procedures are not consistently documented.

---

## Asset Management

The organization does not maintain a centralized inventory of company devices.

---

## AI Usage

Employees have begun using public AI tools independently.

No governance or guidance currently exists.

---

## Overall Assessment

Current operations support a small organization but will become increasingly difficult to manage as the company grows.
