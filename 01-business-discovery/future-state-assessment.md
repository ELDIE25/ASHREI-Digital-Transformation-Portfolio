# Future State Assessment

## Vision

ASHREI will operate as a technology-enabled facilities services organization supported by standardized business processes, secure IT operations and responsible Artificial Intelligence.

---

## IT Operations

A centralized Help Desk manages all incidents and service requests.

---

## Workforce

Every employee follows a documented onboarding process.

---

## Asset Management

Every device is inventoried and assigned to an owner.

---

## AI Governance

Only approved AI tools are used.

AI activities follow documented governance policies.

---

## Business Intelligence

Management dashboards provide real-time operational and performance information.

---

## Overall Vision

Technology, governance and standardized processes enable ASHREI to scale efficiently while maintaining security, compliance and excellent customer service.
