# 1. Executive Summary


ASHREI Facility Services is a planned commercial cleaning and facility services company based in Ottawa, Ontario. The organization currently consists of nine employees and has an ambitious vision to become one of Ottawa's most technology-driven cleaning companies.

Management intends to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) to improve scheduling, customer support, inspections, reporting, quality control, employee training and administrative processes while maintaining strong standards for privacy, cybersecurity and responsible AI use.

This Business Discovery Report summarizes the current business environment, identifies operational and technology challenges, and provides initial recommendations that will guide the Digital Transformation, IT Operations and AI Governance program.

# 2. Business Context

## Organization Overview

ASHREI Facility Services is a planned commercial cleaning and facility services company headquartered in Ottawa, Ontario.

The organization currently consists of **nine employees** and has established an ambitious growth strategy to expand to approximately **40 employees within the next 24 months**.

ASHREI provides commercial cleaning and facility support services, including:

- Commercial office cleaning
- Carpet and upholstery cleaning
- Mattress cleaning
- Floor care
- Move-in and move-out cleaning
- Post-construction cleaning
- Emergency cleaning
- Facility support services

The company's long-term vision is to become one of Ottawa's most technology-driven cleaning companies by integrating digital transformation, structured IT operations, cybersecurity governance and responsible Artificial Intelligence into its daily operations.

The company intends to serve a broad range of clients, including:

- Commercial offices
- Healthcare facilities
- Retail businesses
- Property management companies
- Government contractors

As the business expands, management recognizes that operational growth must be supported by scalable technology, standardized business processes and effective governance.

# 3. Key Business Challenges

The discovery phase identified several operational and technology challenges that should be addressed before the company scales.

The most significant challenges include:

- No centralized IT support process.
- No ticketing system for managing technical issues.
- No documented employee onboarding process.
- No inventory of company laptops, mobile devices or other IT assets.
- Employees are already using AI tools without organizational guidance.
- Business knowledge is not documented or shared consistently.
- No formal governance framework for information management or AI usage.
- Operational activities such as attendance, observations and incidents are not consistently tracked.

These issues increase operational risk and may reduce efficiency, service quality and business scalability.

# 4. Technology Assessment

## Current Technology Strengths

ASHREI has already adopted several cloud-based productivity tools that provide a solid foundation for future digital transformation.

Current technology includes:

- Microsoft 365
- Outlook
- Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Word
- Shared OneDrive folders
- QuickBooks
- Company website

These tools support communication, collaboration, document management and financial administration.

Although the technology foundation is appropriate for a growing organization, additional governance, operational processes and security controls are required before the business can scale effectively.

## Technology Gaps

Despite these strengths, several important capabilities are currently missing:

- No dedicated Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform.
- No Help Desk or Service Desk function.
- No centralized ticketing system.
- No IT asset inventory.
- No AI governance framework.
- No documented cybersecurity procedures.

Addressing these gaps will improve operational efficiency, security and future business growth.

# 6. Initial Recommendations

Based on the Business Discovery phase, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. Implement a centralized IT Service Desk to manage incidents and service requests.

2. Develop an IT Asset Register to improve accountability for company devices.

3. Introduce Microsoft Entra ID governance to strengthen identity and access management.

4. Develop standardized onboarding and offboarding procedures for employees.

5. Create an Artificial Intelligence Acceptable Use Policy before expanding AI adoption.

6. Develop a centralized Knowledge Base to capture operational knowledge and reduce dependency on individuals.

7. Implement Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) to strengthen account security.

8. Design an AI-enabled Help Desk capable of supporting employees while maintaining appropriate governance and human oversight.
