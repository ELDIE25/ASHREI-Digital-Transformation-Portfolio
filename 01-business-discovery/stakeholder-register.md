# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Responsibilities | Business Needs | Current Pain Points | Influence | Engagement Strategy |
|--------------|------------------|----------------|---------------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Founder / CEO | Defines business strategy, approves budgets and oversees company growth. | Business growth, profitability, visibility into operations and compliance. | Limited visibility into IT issues, lack of governance and scalability challenges. | High | Weekly executive updates and strategic decision meetings. |
| Operations Supervisor | Manages daily cleaning operations and employee scheduling. | Efficient scheduling, reliable communication and service quality. | No ticketing system, manual processes and inconsistent reporting. | High | Gather operational requirements and involve in process design. |
| Administrative Assistant | Manages administrative tasks, documentation and customer communications. | Organized documentation, efficient workflows and accurate records. | Manual processes, duplicate work and inconsistent documentation. | Medium | Provide standardized procedures and training. |
| Cleaning Technicians | Deliver cleaning services to clients. | Easy access to work instructions, equipment and technical support. | No Help Desk, undocumented procedures and limited knowledge sharing. | Medium | Design user-friendly processes and provide practical training. |
| Clients | Receive commercial cleaning services. | Reliable service, timely communication and high-quality results. | Delayed communication, inconsistent reporting and limited service visibility. | High | Regular communication, service reports and customer feedback sessions. |
| Future IT Support | Supports users, systems and technology. | Standardized procedures, documented tickets and asset visibility. | No ticketing platform, no knowledge base and no asset inventory. | High | Develop IT Service Management processes and documentation. |
| Finance | Oversees financial operations and reporting. | Accurate financial information and secure business records. | Manual record keeping and limited reporting automation. | Medium | Implement secure documentation and reporting standards. |
| Technology Vendors | Provide software, hardware and technical services. | Clear requirements and effective communication. | Undefined technology roadmap and inconsistent requirements. | Low | Regular vendor reviews and documented requirements. |


## Stakeholder Analysis

The Founder / CEO and Operations Supervisor are considered the primary stakeholders because they have the greatest influence over business decisions and operational success.

Cleaning Technicians are the primary users of the future IT support processes and therefore must be involved when designing practical and easy-to-use solutions.

Clients remain the most important external stakeholders because service quality, communication and trust directly influence business growth and reputation.

The Future IT Support function will become increasingly important as ASHREI expands and introduces additional technology, cybersecurity controls and AI-enabled services.
