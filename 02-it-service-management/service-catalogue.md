# Service Catalogue

**Client:** ASHREI Facility Services  
**Phase:** 02 — IT Service Management  
**Document Owner:** IT Operations  
**Status:** Complete  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Service Catalogue defines the standard IT services available to ASHREI employees. It establishes clear expectations regarding the support provided by the IT Service Desk, promotes consistency in service delivery, and ensures employees know where to request assistance.

---

# Scope

The catalogue applies to all ASHREI employees, supervisors, managers, contractors, and approved third-party users who require access to company technology or IT support services.

---

# Service Objectives

The objectives of the IT Service Catalogue are to:

- Standardize IT support services across the organization.
- Provide employees with a single point of contact for technology issues.
- Improve service quality and response times.
- Ensure incidents and service requests are tracked consistently.
- Support business continuity through reliable IT operations.

---

# Service Categories

## 1. User Account Management

### Description

Support related to user identities and system access.

### Services

- New user account creation
- Password reset
- Account unlock
- Username changes
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enrollment
- Account deactivation
- Role and permission updates

### Typical Users

All employees

---

## 2. Microsoft 365 Support

### Description

Support for Microsoft productivity applications used throughout ASHREI.

### Services

- Outlook email support
- Microsoft Teams support
- OneDrive support
- Microsoft Office troubleshooting
- Shared mailbox access
- Calendar issues
- Teams meeting support

### Typical Users

All employees

---

## 3. Hardware Support

### Description

Support for company-issued devices and peripherals.

### Services

- Laptop troubleshooting
- Desktop troubleshooting
- Printer support
- Monitor setup
- Keyboard and mouse replacement
- Mobile device support
- Docking station issues

### Typical Users

All employees

---

## 4. Software Support

### Description

Support for approved business software.

### Services

- Software installation
- Software updates
- License requests
- Application troubleshooting
- Removal of unsupported software

### Typical Users

All employees

---

## 5. Network & Connectivity

### Description

Support for connectivity and communication services.

### Services

- Wi-Fi issues
- VPN support
- Internet connectivity
- Remote access
- Network printer connectivity

### Typical Users

Office staff and remote employees

---

## 6. Security Services

### Description

Services that help protect company information and systems.

### Services

- MFA support
- Security awareness assistance
- Suspicious email reporting
- Lost or stolen device reporting
- Malware reporting
- Account compromise reporting

### Typical Users

All employees

---

## 7. Employee Lifecycle Support

### Description

Technology services supporting employee onboarding, transfers, and departures.

### Services

- New employee onboarding
- Equipment assignment
- User account provisioning
- Employee offboarding
- Account deactivation
- Equipment return

### Typical Users

Managers, Human Resources, IT

---

## 8. AI Support Services

### Description

Support for approved artificial intelligence tools used within ASHREI.

### Services

- Approved AI tool access
- AI usage guidance
- AI policy questions
- AI incident reporting
- AI access requests
- AI tool approval requests

### Typical Users

Employees using approved AI services

# Service Request Channels

Employees may request support using:

- IT Service Portal
- Company email
- Microsoft Teams
- Telephone
- Walk-up support (when available)

---

# Service Exclusions

The following services are outside the scope of the IT Service Desk:

- Personal devices not approved for business use
- Personal email accounts
- Unlicensed software
- Home network troubleshooting
- Unauthorized AI applications
- Personal cloud storage

---

The effectiveness of IT services will be measured using:

- First Response Time
- Average Resolution Time
- Tickets Resolved Within SLA
- First Contact Resolution Rate
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Ticket Backlog
- Repeat Incident Rate

# Interview Talking Points

This document demonstrates my understanding of how an IT Service Desk organizes and communicates the services it provides to employees. Rather than simply listing technical tasks, the catalogue groups services into business-oriented categories, defines who the services support, and establishes clear expectations for how IT contributes to daily operations.

A service catalogue helps employees know where to request assistance, improves consistency in service delivery, and supports service management practices by clearly defining available IT services.
