# ASHREI Digital Transformation Portfolio

## Overview

This repository documents a 14-day consulting-style portfolio project designed to demonstrate practical skills in:

- IT support and service desk operations
- IT service management
- Microsoft 365 and identity administration
- Cybersecurity governance, risk and compliance
- Artificial intelligence governance
- Business analysis and digital transformation
- Responsible AI product design
- Executive reporting

## Business Scenario

ASHREI Facility Services is an Ottawa-based commercial cleaning and facilities-services business preparing to modernize its operations.

The organization requires reliable IT support, secure information handling, formal risk-management practices and responsible adoption of artificial intelligence.

This portfolio presents the processes, policies, risk assessments, operating procedures and product designs created to support that transformation.
