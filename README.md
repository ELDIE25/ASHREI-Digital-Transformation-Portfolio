## Project Overview

This repository documents a **14-day consulting engagement simulation** created to demonstrate practical, job-ready skills for entry-level positions in:

- IT Support
- Help Desk & IT Operations
- Business Analysis
- Microsoft 365 Administration
- Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)
- AI Governance
- Digital Transformation

The portfolio is structured as if I were part of a consulting team engaged by **ASHREI Facility Services** to design and document the organization's IT modernization and responsible AI adoption strategy.

# Business Scenario

ASHREI Facility Services is a planned commercial cleaning and facilities services company headquartered in **Ottawa, Ontario**.

The company is preparing for rapid growth and requires:

- Structured IT support
- Standardized business processes
- Cybersecurity controls
- Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC)
- Responsible Artificial Intelligence adoption
- Executive reporting
- Digital transformation

This portfolio documents the analysis, recommendations and deliverables developed throughout the engagement.
